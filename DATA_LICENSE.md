# Data licence and attribution

The tables in `data/` and the report in `docs/` are released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
They are derived from the datasets below, which must be credited when the data are reused:

- **US Water Quality Portal** (USGS, US EPA, National Water Quality Monitoring Council), https://www.waterqualitydata.us — public domain.
- **EEA Waterbase – Water Quality ICM 2026**, European Environment Agency — CC BY 4.0.
- **SYKE VESLA water quality data**, Finnish Environment Institute — CC BY 4.0.
- **UNEP GEMStat open subset GFQA v3**, doi:10.5281/zenodo.18459694 — CC BY 4.0.
- **Gippsland Lakes Water Quality Data 1990–2026**, EPA Victoria, DataVic — CC BY 4.0.
- **Environment Canterbury water quality data** (Hilltop service) — CC BY 4.0 (Environment Canterbury data terms).
- **Lewis et al. (2023)**, lake productivity synthesis, EDI edi.1530.1, doi:10.6073/pasta/2cd6628a942de2a8b12d2b19962712a0 — CC BY 4.0.
- **Lakes_cci v2.1** per-lake tables of Shahvaran et al. (ESSD preprint essd-2026-528), doi:10.5281/zenodo.20347115 — CC BY 4.0;
  ESA Lake Climate Change Initiative (Lakes_cci).
- **HydroLAKES / LakeATLAS v1.0** (Messager et al. 2016; Lehner et al. 2022) — CC BY 4.0.

Not redistributed here: local lake observations (Erken, Bosumtwi; project data, only their per-lake results appear in
`data/results`) and COGERH Ceará values (licence not stated; site positions only, in `data/insitu/cogerh_chl_sites.csv`).
